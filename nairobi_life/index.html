NAIROBI LIFE
A realistic street-life survival sim set in Nairobi, Kenya. Real weather (two rainy seasons, cool grey Julys), real KSh prices, real hunger, rent and police. Stay alive.

Real Climate

Long rains (Mar-May), short rains (Oct-Dec), cool dry June-September. Convective afternoon downpours, short equatorial twilight.

Real Economy

Earn as a hawker, mjengo casual labourer, scrap collector or handcart pusher. Pay rent, food, water, clinic in KSh.

Living City

512 NPCs and matatus that rush with traffic and run for cover when it rains. Askaris, thugs and night in Kibera.
